# Mana: hits pay, spells cost

**Date:** 2026-10-17. **Issue:** #60, step 3 of `docs/plans/2026-10-17-vertical-slices.md`. **Built by:** Ben's Claude from the plan and section 8 of `docs/architecture.md` (proposed), with Ben at the keyboard. Ben has not reviewed the numbers yet.

**Status: proposed.** It builds the spell economy the architecture proposes: mana fills when you land hits, like a fighting-game super meter, so playing aggressively feeds the spells. Every number is data on the moves and the fighter, to tune by playing. A guard bar follows in #72, on the same pattern.

## What it does

- **A mana bar** under each player's health bar, in blue, brighter when full.
- **Hits pay.** Every hit that lands pays its attacker the `mana` on its hit data: normals pay most, a spell's own hits pay a little, the super pays nothing. A blocked hit pays nothing. Enemies never gain mana.
- **Spells cost.** A move with a `mana_cost` only starts when the fighter can pay, and pays on starting. When it can't, the next move in the list is tried, so the fireball motion with an empty bar gives a plain light attack, a heavy fireball you can't afford gives a plain heavy, and the super's motion with less than a full bar gives the heavy fireball.
- **The super costs the whole bar.**
- **Where you start:** the melee starts empty, since hits pay; the boss fight and the sandbox start full, so motions can be tried at once. **F7** refills everyone, for testing.

## Numbers to tune (proposed)

| What | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mana bar | 100 | `Fighter.max_mana` |
| Light, second light, air light pay | 6 | `HitBox.mana` in `data/moves/poc/` |
| Heavy, air heavy pay | 10 | the same |
| Fireball, missiles, blast hits pay | 2 light, 3 heavy, per body hit | the same, on `projectile_hit` |
| Super pays | 0 | the same |
| Fireball costs | 20 light, 35 heavy | `Move.mana_cost` |
| Missiles cost | 20 light, 35 heavy | the same |
| Blast costs | 25 light, 40 heavy | the same |
| Super costs | 100 | the same |

So the three-hit combo pays 22, the full combo with the two air hits 38, and a light fireball is about three normals' worth. A heavy special back-to-back needs a combo and a half.

## How it is built

- `HitBox.mana` and `Move.mana_cost` are data. `Body.gain_mana` is a no-op; `Fighter` keeps `mana` and `max_mana` and pays in `_try_moves`, which skips a move it can't afford. A `max_mana` of 0 means no bar and every move free, as for the big dummy, the move preview tool and the older tests. `Combat` pays the attacker of every clean hit, and a projectile's caster (co-op's `owner_of`).
- The bars are on the stage's HUD layer with the health bars, one per player.

## Proposed: choices made while building, to confirm or change

- **Only hits fill mana,** not time. The architecture lists refill over time and cooldowns as alternatives; the plan picked hits.
- **A spell you can't afford falls through to the next move**, rather than doing nothing. It keeps the button responsive, and the fallback is in the move list's order.
- **Spells pay a little back** so a spell that lands on a crowd is not a dead end, but never enough to chain them without normals in between.
- **Mana is kept across waves and arenas** and reset only by R.
- **Enemies have no mana.** The big dummy's brain has no specials; when a boss gets them (#67), its spells can be on cooldowns or on its own bar.
