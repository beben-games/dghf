# A boss: strings, tells, a grab, two phases, a named bar

**Date:** 2026-10-18. **Issue:** #67, step 10 of `docs/plans/2026-10-17-vertical-slices.md`. **Built by:** Ben's Claude from the plan. Ben has not fought it yet.

**Status: proposed.** The boss fight has a boss: a full fighter of a `BossKind` (data in `data/bosses/`), bigger and tougher than the knight, with its moves as data and a brain that plays strings of them, telegraphs its heavies, grabs through a guard, and changes at half health. It is drawn on the plain figure. **Who it is is Open:** the team's round-three characters say who the bosses are (#33); "the Captain" is a placeholder title, not lore.

## What it does

- **The Captain:** 600 health, 1.3 times the knight's size, a slow walk, a long reach. A named bar at the top centre with a mark at the second phase.
- **Strings.** In reach it plays one of its patterns: light, light, heavy; a grab; light, heavy. A string chains on hit like a player's and breaks on a whiff, then it rests. From far it closes in, and now and then shoots.
- **Tells.** The heavy, the grab, the shot and the lunge are telegraphed: a red mark over its head for their whole wind-up (26 to 32 ticks), the time to dodge, hop, or hit first.
- **The grab** goes through a guard without breaking it, holds the knight where it stands for 36 ticks, then throws it: launched, so a follow-up juggle is the boss's.
- **Phase two** at half health: a breath, then shorter rests and new strings with a **lunge**, a telegraphed charge along the lane that chains into a light.
- **The intro and the K.O.:** its name for a second, then FIGHT; when it falls, K.O. and R for a rematch. Rounds are #68.

## How it is built

- `BossKind` is the data. `BossBrain` is an input source like `FighterBrain` for the walking, the facing, the lane following and the guard, but its attacks are not presses: `Fighter.start_move(move)` starts a move by name under the same rules as a press (the cancel windows unless a landed hit frees the fighter, the mana bar, the fall-backs). The brain keeps a `pattern` of move ids and a `step`, and `started` for tests.
- `HitBox.grab`: no guard stops it and none breaks; `Body.take_hit` holds the target in hitstun with no knockback and throws it when the hold ends (`held()` while it lasts). `Move.telegraphed` and `Fighter.telegraphing()` give the tell, drawn by `FighterView`.
- `Body.size` multiplies the lanes' scale for the figure and its boxes, so a bigger boss hits and is hit bigger.
- The stage's boss fight makes the boss as one of the big dummies, so F4 and F5 drive it, and shows its bar, the intro and the K.O.

## Proposed: choices made while building, to confirm or change

- **Strings by name, not by presses.** `FighterBrain` presses buttons so a dummy plays like a player; the boss starts its moves by name, which makes patterns plain data. Both go through the same rules.
- **The grab holds the target in place** rather than at the boss's hand, and the throw is a launch, so the boss can juggle. No tech or mash-out.
- **A whiff breaks the string.** A boss that commits to its full string regardless is the alternative, and easier to read.
- **One boss, placeholder-named,** on the plain figure in its colour. The character workflow in `docs/art.md` gives it a look once the team says who it is.
