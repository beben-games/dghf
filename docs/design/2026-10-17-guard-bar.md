# The guard bar: blocking drains it, hits refill it, at zero the guard breaks

**Date:** 2026-10-17. **Issue:** #72. **Agreed with:** Ben, in a brainstorm with his Claude on 2026-10-17, alongside mana (#60). The rules below are Ben's; the numbers are Claude's, to tune by playing.

**Status: proposed.** It makes the guard (#41, proposed) breakable, and sizes the bar per character as data: that is how classes come about. A tank has a much bigger guard bar, to keep a swarm or a boss busy while the rest of the team blasts. Who the three characters are is **Open** in the game bible, so the classes go to the weekly meeting.

## What it does

- **A guard bar** under the mana bar, white. A blocked hit drains it by the hit's damage instead of touching health.
- **It comes back fast when landing hits** (a fixed amount per hit), **and slowly when not being hit**: after a moment without taking or blocking anything, it trickles back.
- **At zero the guard breaks.** The hit that empties the bar lands in full, as a launcher: the player flies, can be juggled like any launched body (a boss gets its combo), and lands knocked down with the usual get-up. The screen shakes.
- **Below a quarter of the bar the guard can't be raised**: a broken guard has to come back first, so a broken tank fights or dodges its way out rather than turtling.
- **F8** gives player 1 a tank's bar, three times bigger, to feel the difference before a second character exists.

## Numbers to tune (proposed)

| What | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Guard bar | 100 (tank preset 300) | `Fighter.max_guard`, `PocStage.GUARD_BAR` and `TANK_GUARD` |
| A blocked hit drains | its damage (a small fry's jab 5, a light 10, a heavy 22) | `HitBox.damage` |
| A hit landed refills | 15 | `Fighter.guard_per_hit` |
| Trickle, after this long without being hit | 60 ticks, then 1 every 4 ticks (15 a second) | `guard_regen_delay`, `guard_regen`, `guard_regen_every` |
| The guard can't be raised below | 25 | `Fighter.guard_floor` |
| The breaking hit's launch, when it has none of its own | 22 pixels a tick | `Fighter.break_launch` |
| Shake on a break | 12 pixels | `Combat.BREAK_SHAKE` |

So twenty jabs break a fresh bar, seven hits landed refill it, and an empty bar comes back on its own in about seven seconds of not being hit; a tank's takes sixty jabs and twenty hits.

## How it is built

- `Fighter.max_guard`, `guard` and the refill numbers are data on the fighter. 0 means no bar: the guard never breaks, as for the big dummy and the older tests.
- `Body.blocks_hit(hit, direction)` is what `Combat` asks now, in place of `blocks(direction)`: a fighter blocks a hit it faces while its bar can take it. When it can't, the hit lands through `take_hit`, which sees the fighter was guarding, empties the bar, marks `just_broke`, and gives the hit the break's launch if it has none.
- `Body.on_hit_landed(hit)` replaces the mana call: a clean hit pays its attacker mana and guard in one place.

## Proposed: choices made while building, to confirm or change

- **A blocked hit drains its damage.** No separate guard-damage number until a move needs one; the guard breaker planned in #61 can then be a move with a large one, or one that ignores the guard.
- **A floor of a quarter** rather than a fixed recovery time, so hitting back is what brings the guard back.
- **Hits from behind are not breaks**, they were never blocked: a hit in the back while guarding is a plain hit, as before.
- **The tank preset only changes the bar.** Speed, health and moves stay; a real tank would differ in those too, with its own move list, through the character workflow (#67).
